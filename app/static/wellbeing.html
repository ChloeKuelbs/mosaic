What the research can—and can't—tell you.

Age-based search results do not establish that you need any particular vitamin, supplement, screening, or exercise program. Nutrient requirements, deficiency risk, and evidence of benefit are different questions. Follow original sources and discuss personal decisions with a qualified clinician.
