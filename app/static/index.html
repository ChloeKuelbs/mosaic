How Mosaic works

Mosaic brings together live ClinicalTrials.gov registrations and recent PubMed-indexed publications. Ask Mosaic analyzes selected trial registry records, links the original sources, and acknowledges when evidence is insufficient. Registry descriptions are not proof of treatment effectiveness, and research listings are not medical advice.
